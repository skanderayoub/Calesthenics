# 12-Week Calisthenics Program — Adapted

**Starting point:** 4 strict pull-ups, 10 dips, 66 kg / 174 cm, age 29
**Goal:** muscle + aesthetics, plus handstand / L-sit / eventually muscle-up
**Equipment:** pull-up + dip station at home, calisthenics park
**Frequency:** 4 days/week

---

## 0. Get these two things first

| Item | Cost | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Resistance band set (loop bands) | ~€20 | Pull-up assistance, face pulls, curls, rows |
| Sturdy backpack | free | Weighted dips and pull-ups later — this is how you keep progressing past bodyweight |

Bodyweight-only stops working eventually. The backpack is what prevents that.

### No rings needed

Every exercise in this program is done on a bar, dip station, floor, or band. Nothing here requires rings.

If you ever want them later (~€30), they mainly add three things — and each has a bar alternative you're already doing:

| Ring exercise | What it gives you | Bar/floor alternative in this program |
|---|---|---|
| Ring rows | Rowing volume, shoulder-friendly | Inverted rows on a park bar |
| Ring dips | Extra stability demand, deeper range | Straight-bar dips (Phase 3), or weighted parallel-bar dips |
| Ring push-ups / flies | Chest stretch under load | Deficit push-ups — hands on two low, stable objects so your chest drops below hand level |

Rings are a nice-to-have, not a requirement. Don't wait on them to start.

---

## 1. The progression rule

This is what the Reddit program was missing, and it matters more than the exercise selection.

**Double progression.** Every exercise has a set count and a rep range.

1. Stay at the same variation until you hit the **top of the rep range on every set**, two sessions in a row.
2. Then progress: add load (backpack) if possible, otherwise move to the harder variation.
3. When you progress, you drop back to the bottom of the rep range. That's normal.

**Log every session.** A note on your phone is enough — exercise, sets, reps. If you don't log it, you can't progress it.

**Leave 1–2 reps in the tank** on most sets. Going to total failure every set on 4 days/week will bury your recovery. Last set of an exercise can go close to failure.

**Never advance phases by calendar.** Advance when you hit the criteria listed in section 5.

## 2. Weekly structure

| Day | Session |
|---|---|
| Mon | Upper A (pull emphasis) |
| Tue | Lower + Core |
| Wed | Off — walk, mobility, or nothing |
| Thu | Upper B (push emphasis) |
| Fri | Lower + Core |
| Sat / Sun | Off. Optional 10 min handstand practice — it's skill, not training |

Skill work goes **first**, right after the warm-up, when you're fresh. Never at the end — the Reddit program had this backwards.

**Rest between sets:** 2–3 minutes on the hard compound work (pull-ups, dips). This will feel like too long. It isn't. Short rests are the main reason people "get tired quickly" and then stall — you're limiting yourself by fatigue rather than strength, which is the wrong stimulus for what you want.

## 3. Warm-up (same every session, ~7 min)

1. 2 min easy cardio — jog in place, jumping jacks, skipping
2. Arm circles ×10 each direction
3. Band or towel shoulder dislocates ×10
4. Scapular pull-ups ×8 · scapular push-ups ×8
5. 10 bodyweight squats · 10 hip hinges · world's greatest stretch ×5/side
6. One light warm-up set of your first exercise

## 4. Phase 1 — Weeks 1–4 (approximately)

### Upper A — pull emphasis

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Wall handstand (chest to wall) | 4 × 20–30 s | Skill work. Stop before failure |
| 2 | Pull-ups | 6 × 2 | Rest 2–3 min. Yes, only 2 reps — read the note below |
| 3 | Dips | 4 × 5–6 | Rest 2 min. Full range, controlled |
| 4 | Inverted rows | 3 × 8–12 | Park bar at hip height. Squeeze at the top |
| 5 | Pike push-ups | 3 × 6–10 | Feet elevated once easy |
| 6 | Band curls | 3 × 12–15 | Superset with #7 |
| 7 | Band face pulls | 3 × 15 | Shoulder health + rear delts |

### Lower + Core

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tuck L-sit | 5 × 10–15 s | On dip bars or floor. Skill work |
| 2 | Bulgarian split squat | 4 × 8–12 / leg | Backpack once you pass 12 |
| 3 | Nordic curl negatives | 3 × 5 | Or single-leg RDL 3×10/leg if you can't anchor your feet |
| 4 | Step-ups (knee-height) | 3 × 10–12 / leg | Slow, no pushing off the back foot |
| 5 | Single-leg calf raise | 3 × 12–20 | Full stretch at the bottom |
| 6 | Hanging knee raises | 3 × 8–12 | No swinging |
| 7 | Hollow body hold | 3 × 20–30 s | Lower back stays pressed down |

### Upper B — push emphasis

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Wall handstand + shoulder taps | 4 × 20–30 s | Add taps when the hold is solid |
| 2 | Dips | 5 × 5 | Rest 2–3 min. Heavier day |
| 3 | Chin-ups (underhand) | 4 × 3 | Easier than pull-ups — that's the point |
| 4 | Push-ups, feet elevated | 4 × 8–15 | 2 seconds down, 1 up |
| 5 | Inverted rows, feet elevated | 3 × 8–12 | |
| 6 | Diamond push-ups | 3 × 8–12 | Triceps |
| 7 | Pull-up negatives | 3 × 3 | Jump up, lower over 5 s. Finisher |

### Why 6 × 2 pull-ups instead of 3 × 5

At a 4-rep max, sets of 5–8 mean you fail on set two and grind ugly reps for the rest. Six clean doubles gives you 12 quality reps instead of 8 sloppy ones, with far less fatigue. Volume drives progress here, not proximity to failure.

**Weekly:** add one rep to one set. Week 1: 2,2,2,2,2,2 → Week 2: 3,2,2,2,2,2 → and so on. When you reach 6 × 3, retest your max. You'll be surprised.

## 5. Phase 2 — start when you hit ALL of these

- 6 strict pull-ups
- 15 dips
- 20 elevated push-ups
- 40 s wall handstand

**Changes:**

- **Pull-ups** → 5 × 3–5, then start adding backpack weight
- **Dips** → 4 × 8–12, add backpack weight when you clear 12
- Add **archer push-ups** 3 × 6–8/side in place of diamond push-ups
- Add **wall handstand negatives** — walk in close, lower slowly to head, press back up
- **L-sit** → advanced tuck (hips open more), 5 × 10 s
- **Bulgarian split squats** → weighted backpack, 4 × 6–10/leg
- Legs: add **assisted pistol squat** progressions (box squat to lower and lower surfaces)
- Core: **hanging knee raises → straight leg raises**

---

## 6. Phase 3 — start when you hit ALL of these

- 10 strict pull-ups (or 6 with +10 kg)
- 15 dips (or 8 with +10 kg)
- 15 s tuck L-sit
- Comfortable 60 s wall handstand

**This is when the muscle-up becomes a reasonable goal.** Add:

- **Explosive pull-ups** — chest to bar, 5 × 3
- **Straight-bar dips** — 4 × 6–10 (harder than parallel bars, and the muscle-up needs them)
- **Band-assisted muscle-ups** — 4 × 3, focus on the transition, not the rep
- **Free-standing handstand** — kick-up practice against a wall, then away from it
- **Full L-sit** — one leg straight, then both
- **Pistol squats** — 4 × 5–8/leg
- Keep loading dips and pull-ups with the backpack. This is where most of the muscle comes from now.

---

## 7. Deload

Every 6–8 weeks, or whenever your numbers go backwards two sessions in a row: take one week at **half the sets, same reps**. Keep skill practice. You'll come back stronger — this is a feature, not lost time.

---

## 8. Nutrition — the actual limiting factor for you

At 66 kg with your build, training is not what's holding back muscle growth. Food is.

- **Calories:** roughly +250 above maintenance. Target ~0.2–0.3 kg gained per month. If the scale hasn't moved in three weeks, eat more.
- **Protein:** ~110–130 g/day. Spread across 3–4 meals.
- **Practical:** easiest levers are whole milk, eggs, oats, nuts, olive oil, rice. Liquid calories are the friend of anyone who struggles to eat.
- **Sleep:** 7–9 hours. More impactful than any exercise selection choice in this document.

Slow gain means most of it is muscle. Fast gain means most of it isn't.

---

## 9. If you're still "getting tired quickly" after 3 weeks

Two different problems, two different fixes:

- **Breathing / heart rate spikes** → conditioning. Add two 20–30 min easy walks, runs, or bike rides on off days. Won't interfere with muscle growth at this intensity.
- **Muscles burning out, arms giving up** → you're resting too little between sets. Time your rests with a phone timer for two weeks and see what changes.
